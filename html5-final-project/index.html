About Yenepoya University

Discover Yenepoya University, its academic programs, campus facilities and opportunities for a brighter future. This site introduces the university in a clear and accessible way for prospective students and their families.

Programs and Opportunities

Students can explore a range of academic paths and use campus support to build knowledge, skills and confidence.

  • Academic programs across health, science, management and related fields
  • Library and study facilities that support independent and group learning
  • Campus activities that encourage teamwork, leadership and community
  • Guidance and opportunities for a brighter future after graduation

Campus Life and Learning

Campus life combines study, facilities and shared experiences. The library is a quiet place for reading and research, while classrooms and common spaces support discussion and collaboration.
