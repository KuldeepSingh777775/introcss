Nature Photo Gallery

This gallery brings together nine photographs of fields, forests, lakes, mountains, and the shore. Each photograph was chosen to show a different kind of light and landscape, from quiet morning fog to bright sunset color.

The page is built mobile-first. On a phone the photographs appear in a single column, on a tablet they appear in two columns, and on a large screen they appear in three columns, with every third photograph spanning two columns. The layout also responds to reduced-motion and dark color scheme settings.
